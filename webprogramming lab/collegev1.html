About us

The College of Engineering Chengannur commonly known as CEC, is an engineering institute in the state of Kerala, India, that was established by the Government of Kerala under the aegis of the Institute of Human Resources Development in 1993. The college is located in Chengannur, Alappuzha.The College of Engineering Chengannur was established in the year 1993, with a vision to create engineers having the drive, skill, and confidence to become the pioneers of tomorrow. The college was set up under the auspices of the Institute of Human Resources and Development (IHRD) and is recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. It is affiliated to the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).

courses offered

The admission process for the College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) is entirely handled through online, state-level counseling based on entrance exam ranks rather than direct applications to the college. For regular B.Tech admissions, candidates must clear the KEAM exam and participate in the CEE Kerala Online Counseling using the college code CHN, while diploma holders seeking lateral entry must clear the LET exam through LBS Centre counseling, and NRI applicants can apply directly via the IHRD Kerala website based on their 12th-grade marks. For postgraduate courses, M.Tech applicants are selected through the DTE Kerala portal based on their GATE scores, whereas MCA aspirants must qualify for the Kerala MCA Entrance Exam and apply through the LBS Centre portal. Once a seat is officially allotted through any of these online channels, every candidate must visit the CEC campus in person to verify their documents, pay the academic fees, and finalize their admission.
