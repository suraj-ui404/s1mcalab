ONAM

Onam is the official cultural and harvest festival of kerala, celebrated as a vibrand ten-day evend during the Malayalam month of Chingam. The festival marks the joyous annual homecoming of the legendary, benevolent Asura King Mahabali, whose reign is remembered as a golden era of complete equality, peace, and properity. The festivities begin on the star Atham and peak on the final, most auspicious day known as Thiruvonam.

During these ten days, homws are adorned with pookalam, which are intricate, colorful floral carpets laid at entrances to welcome the spirit of Kind Mahabali. A central highlighting of the celebration is the banana leaf. The festival is also celebrated with spectacular community events, including the thrilling vallam kali (sanke boat race ) held on kerala's rivers, and lively traditional folk arts such as the pulikali (tiger dance) and kaikottikali dance performance.
